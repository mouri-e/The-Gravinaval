# The Gravinaval

The Gravinaval is a maze-style puzzle 3D platformer that takes place on an alien spacecraft. The main
character, an astronaut miner, must find their way through winding paths in many rooms of a spaceship that
is manned entirely by robotics and avoid the implements used by it to crush and extract minerals. With the
aid of an AI buddy that lets them flip gravity and power up their pickaxe, they can smash and flip through
each level to reach the control room and fly back to their home planet.
